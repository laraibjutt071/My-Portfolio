01About

I work across the applied ML stack — from cleaning tabular health data and training classical models, to fine-tuning generative and retrieval pipelines with LLMs. What ties my projects together is trust: a diabetes model is only useful if a clinician can see why it flagged a patient, and a chatbot answering medical questions is only safe if its answers are grounded in real sources.

My recent work spans explainable AI for image generation, sensor-based activity recognition, and RAG-based medical chatbots — alongside lighter product builds like a wallpaper app. Code for most of it is on GitHub.
